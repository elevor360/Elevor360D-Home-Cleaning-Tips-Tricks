Elevor360D Home Cleaning Tips & Tricks V1
========================================
A dedicated home-cleaning app with 50 practical cleaning tips, room categories,
step-by-step guidance, safety notes, favourites, personal tips, a manual
cleaning schedule/checklist, and backup/restore. Includes offline PWA support.

GITHUB:
Create Elevor360D-Home-Cleaning-Tips-Tricks repository and upload index.html,
app.js, tips.json, manifest.webmanifest, icon.svg, sw.js and advertising/ folder.
Do NOT upload Elevor360D_Cleaning_Tips_Manager_V1.html; keep it on your computer.

MANAGER:
Open manager locally in Chrome, import tips.json, add/edit/delete tips,
export tips.json and replace the file on GitHub. The app uses network-first
updates for tips.json with offline fallback.

ADVERTISING:
Uses advertising/adverts.json and advertising/images/ with centred image,
title and previous/next buttons, compatible with existing advert folder layout.

SAFETY:
Never mix bleach with vinegar, ammonia or other chemicals. Always read labels,
check material compatibility, ventilate and keep chemicals out of reach.
The tips are general guidance and are not individually tested on all surfaces.
